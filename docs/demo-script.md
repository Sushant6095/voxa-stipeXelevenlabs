# Voxa — 60-Second Demo Video Script

> Three "wow" moments. Real phone call, real Stripe meter, real WhatsApp. Under 60 seconds.

## Shot list

### 0:00–0:05 — Hook
- **Setting:** outside, handheld, walking down a Bengaluru street
- **Visual:** you, phone in hand
- **Line:** "Every small business in India misses calls. I built an AI receptionist that fixes that — and bills by the minute via Stripe."
- **Cut:** match-cut to phone screen as you swipe to the dialer

### 0:05–0:22 — Demo Act 1 (the call)
- **Setting:** at a coffee shop table
- **Visual:** close-up on phone screen showing the dial pad → outgoing call → connected
- **Audio sequence:**
  - Agent (cloned Hindi voice): *"Namaste, Sharma Dental Clinic, kaise madad kar sakti hoon?"*
  - You: *"Kal subah 11 baje appointment chahiye, root canal ke liye."*
  - Agent: *"Bilkul. 11 AM tomorrow. Aapka naam aur number?"*
  - You: *"Sushant, 98765 43210."*
  - Agent: *"Booked. WhatsApp pe abhi confirmation mil jayegi."*
- **Visual punctuation:** phone buzzes — cut to WhatsApp notification banner with calendar invite

### 0:22–0:38 — Demo Act 2 (the dashboard + Stripe meter)
- **Setting:** laptop on cafe table
- **Visual:** split-screen
  - **Left:** Voxa dashboard with the call streaming live, transcript building
  - **Right:** Stripe Dashboard → Customer → Upcoming invoice
- **Beat at 0:25:** meter event lands → ₹45 added to upcoming invoice (visible on Stripe side)
- **Beat at 0:32:** quick cut to lead score appearing in dashboard: *"9/10 — high intent — root canal — book follow-up"*

### 0:38–0:52 — Demo Act 3 (onboarding speedrun)
- **Voiceover:** *"Setup takes 60 seconds."*
- **Visual sequence (time-lapse):**
  - Stripe Checkout completed (card 4242...)
  - 60-second voice recording in browser
  - Phone number appearing on screen
  - You dialing that number from your phone
  - Agent answering in your cloned voice
- **Cut beat:** smash-cut to close

### 0:52–0:60 — Close
- **Setting:** back to outside or coffee shop close-up
- **Voiceover:** *"Voxa. AI receptionist for Indian SMBs. Stripe usage-based billing, ElevenLabs voice. Try it at voxa.app."*
- **On-screen text:** `@stripe @elevenlabsio #ElevenHacks` + Voxa logo
- **End frame:** clean Voxa logo + tagline ("Never miss a call.")

## Editing rules

- 60s max — cut every line that doesn't earn its place
- Burned-in captions on every spoken line (CapCut auto-captions, manually checked)
- Music bed at 15% volume — use ElevenLabs Music with prompt: *"upbeat tech, synth, 90 bpm, optimistic"*
- Vertical 9:16 cut for IG Reels / TikTok
- Horizontal 16:9 cut for X / LinkedIn
- Cover image: frame from 0:25 (split-screen) — Stripe meter event landing is the hero moment

## Pre-flight checklist (must complete BEFORE filming)

- [ ] Demo phone number works — call it now
- [ ] Cloned voice loaded on demo agent
- [ ] Friend playing customer briefed on the line
- [ ] Coffee shop scouted (lighting + low background noise)
- [ ] Phone charged + cable
- [ ] Laptop charged + Voxa dashboard + Stripe Dashboard open
- [ ] Backup phone ready
- [ ] WhatsApp sandbox: you, friend, demo owner all opted in (within 72h)
- [ ] Cover image draft ready
- [ ] Voxa logo PNG handy

## Voiceover takes (record before filming, sync in edit)

If your filmed audio is rough, record these clean voiceovers separately:

1. *"Every small business in India misses calls. I built an AI receptionist that fixes that — and bills by the minute via Stripe."*
2. *"Setup takes 60 seconds."*
3. *"Voxa. AI receptionist for Indian SMBs. Stripe usage-based billing, ElevenLabs voice. Try it at voxa.app."*

Tip: generate a polished voiceover via ElevenLabs (use a different voice from the Hindi agent) and lay it under the visuals. Saves the day if on-location audio sucks.

## If something goes wrong on shoot day

- **Phone fails to ring:** swap to backup number. If both fail, use a screen recording of an earlier successful call as B-roll.
- **WhatsApp slow:** pre-stage a delivered message and shoot the "phone buzzing → notification appears" beat as a separate clip.
- **Voice clone sounds off:** swap to the default Hindi voice. Note in caption.
- **Stripe meter doesn't show:** show the API request log instead.

The video is the deliverable. Don't let perfect be the enemy of shipped.
